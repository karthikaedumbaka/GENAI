Beneath the sweep of Himalayan snow,
Where Ganga and Yamuna ceaselessly flow,
A land awakens with morning light,
India — radiant, eternal, bright.

From desert sands where camels tread,
To oceans vast, in blue and red,
From lush green fields of Bengal’s plain,
To Western ghats washed by monsoon rain.

A thousand tongues, a million songs,
Whispers of saints, and temple gongs,
Mosques that echo the call to prayer,
Church bells ringing through crisp night air.

She wears her past like sacred thread,
Of rishis wise, of kings who bled,
Ashoka’s wheel and Akbar’s dream,
Echo still like a timeless stream.

In caves of Ajanta, stories unfold,
On Khajuraho’s stones, tales bold,
The Taj Mahal, love’s marble flame,
Etched forever in history’s name.

She is the silence of Bodh Gaya’s tree,
The rhythm of dhol in festivity,
She is the Rigveda’s ancient hymn,
And Bollywood’s dance, alive and dim.

Her freedom rose through cries and chains,
Salt on the shore, blood on the plains,
A tricolor soared, a promise was sown,
A nation reborn, a land its own.

O India, vast mosaic rare,
Of joy and sorrow, hope and care,
Where past and future clasp their hands,
And walk together across her lands.

She feeds the world with spice and grain,
She heals the soul with mantra’s refrain,
She sends her minds to reach the skies,
Yet roots remain where her spirit lies.

For every wound, she finds her song,
For every fall, she rises strong,
In her heart — unbroken flame,
India forever, by any name.

And when the sun sets in saffron glow,
On riverbanks where children grow,
A prayer unites both young and old:
“Bharat — a story forever told.”
